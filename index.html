CRAFTING WORLDS

I'm a Senior Game Developer with 8+ years of experience transforming complex ideas into polished, engaging interactive experiences. My career spans multiplayer strategy games, AI-powered photo booths, holographic installations, and real-time multiplayer systems deployed across mobile and large-scale displays.

Currently at Props Box Entertainment (Riyadh), I integrate cutting-edge AI — from Gemini Veo for video generation to Canon Camera SDK for real-time hardware interaction — with Unity to create unforgettable entertainment experiences.

Whether it's architecting a Nakama-based real-time matchmaking system or designing a monetization funnel that boosted retention by 120%, I bring both deep technical skill and a player-first mindset to every project.
